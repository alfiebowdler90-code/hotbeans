General Requirements for All Roles

Regardless of the specific role, we look for the following in all applicants:

Essential

  • Genuine interest in web development
  • Basic understanding of HTML and CSS
  • Ability to learn quickly and ask for help when needed
  • Good written and verbal communication
  • Right to work in the UK

Desirable (but not required)

  • Relevant qualification (degree, HND, bootcamp certificate)
  • Personal projects or portfolio to share
  • Familiarity with version control (Git)
  • Experience working in an agile team
  • Any freelance or voluntary web work
