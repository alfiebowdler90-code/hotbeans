About HotBeans

HotBeans Web is a Gloucester-based web development company founded in 2014. We specialise in building bespoke web applications, e-commerce platforms, and digital tools for clients across the UK.

Our trainee programme takes motivated individuals — whether fresh from education or changing careers — and gives them the real-world experience needed to become professional web developers. Trainees work alongside senior staff on live client projects from day one.
