About HotBeans Web

Learn about who we are, what we do, and how we're building the next generation of web developers.

Our Story

HotBeans Web was founded in 2014 by brothers Tom and David Hartley, who after years working at large digital agencies grew tired of seeing talented junior developers get overlooked in favour of expensive contract hires. Their vision was simple: build a web development company that treats talent development as a core part of the business, not an afterthought.

Starting with just four employees in a small office above a coffee shop in Gloucester city centre, HotBeans has grown into a team of 38 people delivering web solutions to clients ranging from local SMEs to national retailers and public sector organisations.

Our trainee programme, launched in 2016, has placed over 42 junior developers into permanent roles — the majority of whom are still with us today. We believe the best developers aren't always the ones with the longest CVs, and we build our hiring process around demonstrating that belief.

What We Do

We deliver end-to-end web development services across a range of industries.

  • Bespoke Web Applications Custom-built tools and platforms tailored to client workflows.
  • E-Commerce Development WooCommerce, Shopify, and custom cart solutions.
  • CMS & Content Platforms WordPress, Craft CMS, and headless solutions.
  • API Development & Integration RESTful APIs, third-party integrations, and data pipelines.
  • Maintenance & Support Ongoing support contracts and managed hosting solutions.

How We Work

Our delivery process follows a structured, iterative approach.

  1. Discovery — Requirements gathering and project scoping workshops with clients.
  2. Design — Wireframes and interactive prototypes reviewed with stakeholders.
  3. Development — Agile sprints with weekly demos and regular client check-ins.
  4. Testing — QA, cross-browser testing, accessibility checks and performance audits.
  5. Launch — Staged rollout with monitoring, documentation and handover training.
  6. Support — Post-launch maintenance with defined SLAs.
